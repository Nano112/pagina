# About

Field Notes is written by R. Vasquez, who maintains a handful of command-line tools nobody has
heard of and one that four people depend on.

This page is listed under `nav` in `article.yaml`, which on a blog means it is a standalone page
rather than a post. It has no date, it never appears in the archive on the front page, it is absent
from the feed, and the older/newer arrows at the foot of a post do not lead here.

There is no comment box, and there is no plan for one.
